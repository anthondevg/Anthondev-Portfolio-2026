## The problem

CoquitosTravels needed one product that could help travelers discover and
enquire about tourism packages in Venezuela while giving the internal team the
tools to manage destinations, packages, reservations, and commercial contacts.

## The product

The public site presents featured packages, categories, and destinations. A
searchable catalogue lets visitors filter by destination or category and sort
packages by date or price. Each package has a dedicated page with its gallery,
itinerary, route map, practical information, and frequently asked questions.

Visitors can contact the team through WhatsApp or submit an enrolment request
from the site. Availability takes pending and confirmed reservations into
account. Prices are shown in USD, with an optional reference conversion to
bolívares.

## Administration

The protected admin area uses Supabase Auth and provides a dashboard for
tracking packages, destinations, and reservations. The team can create and
update destinations and packages, including images, itineraries, routes,
categories, FAQs, and publication status.

Reservations are grouped by package and can be created manually or managed as
pending, confirmed, or cancelled. The platform also includes a lightweight CRM
for accommodation, restaurant, and activity contacts, with status filters and
quick editing.

## Product considerations

The experience is responsive and available in Spanish and English. Public and
administrative flows include loading and error states, plus confirmation and
error notifications for important actions.
