# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary professional audience is recruiters and hiring managers evaluating
Anthony Gonzalez for remote AI and full-stack engineering opportunities. The
portfolio also serves the general public, including potential collaborators,
peers, and people interested in Anthony's work.

Visitors need to understand what Anthony can build, how he thinks about
business and product problems, and whether his experience is relevant to an
opportunity or collaboration.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Anthony's AI engineering, full-stack product work, and
approach to creating digital solutions. Its purpose is to turn professional
interest into informed evaluation and direct contact.

Success means that visitors understand Anthony as someone who can contribute
beyond implementation: he understands business logic and wants to participate
in shaping digital solutions from their underlying need through delivery.

## Positioning

Anthony is an AI full-stack engineer who combines hands-on implementation with
an understanding of business logic and a desire to help create the solution,
not merely code a predefined specification.

## Operating Context

The portfolio is an English-language, public website. Visitors can review a
homepage overview, selected case studies, capabilities, experience, and contact
information. Anthony is based in San Fernando de Apure, Venezuela, and is
seeking remote opportunities.

Case studies are stored as local MDX content and published on dedicated work
routes. The site is designed for deployment as a Next.js application.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The portfolio covers AI systems, product engineering, and full-stack
  software delivery.
- Current implementation references LLM applications, AI agents, RAG systems,
  evaluation, TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, APIs, Python, PostgreSQL,
  cloud platforms, and observability. These remain repository claims until
  Anthony confirms his final capability list.
- Project stories must use verified information and outcomes. Confidential,
  estimated, or placeholder metrics must not be presented as real results.
- Work history, résumé details, and the current case-study narratives are
  placeholders awaiting verified content.
- The website must remain responsive and usable without WebGL or motion.

## Product Principles

1. Connect engineering decisions to the business need and the value delivered.
2. Show participation in creating solutions, not only executing specifications.
3. Make Anthony's thinking and individual contribution clear through real
   project evidence.
4. Keep every professional claim specific, credible, and verifiable.
5. Give both specialist and general-public visitors a clear path from initial
   interest to deeper work and contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The experience must support keyboard navigation, semantic landmarks, visible
focus states, reduced-motion preferences, and a functional non-WebGL fallback.
